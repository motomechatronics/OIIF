# Statement of Origin of the OIIF Proposal

**Title:** OIIF — Open Industrial Innovation Framework  
**Original conception and formulation:** **Salvatore Volpe**  
**First public version:** `v1.0.0`, **to be assigned only upon actual publication**  
**Publication date:** *not yet published — do not enter a backdated date*  
**Persistent identifier (optional DOI):** *to be added after an actual deposit*

OIIF is presented here as a proposal for an open approach to promoting interoperability between innovation processes involving universities, companies, and other organisations. The proposal includes Industrial Innovation Proposals (IIPs), a development and independent-validation process, a vision of a common European repository, and principles for governance and maintenance.

**This statement records the formulation of the proposal in its published version and the person presenting it as their original conception. It does not claim that every underlying principle is absolutely original, nor does it constitute legal certification of priority.**

The purpose of publication is to make the proposal accessible to the community so that it can be studied, criticised, developed, and, if useful, adopted. Subsequent modifications should be attributable to their respective contributors. The record of the founding version remains distinct from the technical and institutional responsibilities that others may assume in the future.

The project is not conceived as exclusive property to be asserted against the community. Freedom to develop independent versions must nevertheless be distinguished from acceptance of modifications into the official branch or registry, which will be governed by transparent review rules.

**Signed:** Salvatore Volpe
