# OIIF — Open Industrial Innovation Framework

**An open proposal for improving interoperability between innovation processes in universities and industry.**

**Original conception and formulation:** Salvatore Volpe.  
**Status:** Founding proposal, version 1.0.0; not an approved standard or a validated methodology.
**Public repository:** https://github.com/motomechatronics/OIIF
**Licence for the texts:** Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0).

## The Proposal in Brief

OIIF starts with a question: can difficulties in linking and reusing innovation results and processes across independent organisations be reduced through **shared conventions confined to interfaces**, without imposing a uniform way of working on universities or companies?

The proposal introduces **Industrial Innovation Proposals (IIPs)** as open documents for describing, discussing, experimenting with, and — where justified — validating reusable conventions. A **common European repository** would support discovery, version control, and possible voluntary adoption.

It has not been demonstrated that this method produces a net economic benefit. The documents also identify limitations, costs, and conditions that need to be tested.

## Origin and Community Continuity

The first public version will document a **dated, attributed proposal**, not a claim of absolute originality. The project is intended for community development, with recognition of subsequent contributors' work. See [STATEMENT-OF-ORIGIN.md](STATEMENT-OF-ORIGIN.md).

**Open does not mean indiscriminate acceptance:** proposals and revisions may be freely discussed, but inclusion in the official version requires review and reasoned decisions. The governance described here is a **proposal, not an established structure**. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and Part I, §7.

## What This Repository Is Not

- It is not an application for a funding call and does not reproduce any call's objectives or KPIs.
- It is not a catalogue of validated IIPs: IIP-001, IIP-002, and IIP-003 are conceptual examples.
- It is neither an existing standards body nor an established foundation.
- It does not require disclosure of confidential industrial information.
