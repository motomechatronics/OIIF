# Contributing to OIIF — Proposed Initial Rules

> **Provisional status.** These rules govern review of documents in the initial repository. They are neither community bylaws nor the articles of incorporation of a foundation, nor do they represent an established approval system for standards.

## Welcome

Contributions that help determine whether OIIF addresses real problems are welcome: references to prior approaches, criticism, use cases, comparative data, cost–benefit analysis, documentation improvements, governance proposals, and new IIP hypotheses.

## Submitting a Contribution

1. Before suggesting a new convention, describe the problem and the solutions already available.
2. Explain the context, expected benefits, limitations, and how outcomes could be tested.
3. Clearly identify what information may be published; do not upload trade secrets or personal data without appropriate authorisation.
4. Propose changes through an issue or pull request, explaining what changes and why.
5. When modifying existing texts or IIPs, cite sources and credit previous contributors; avoid unsupported claims of validation.

## Review and Acceptance

Proposals should be assessed for relevance, clarity, respect for organisational autonomy, verifiable usefulness, compatibility, additional costs, and possible alternatives. Initial maintainers may accept a proposal, request changes, or reject it, **providing reasons for their decision**. No author has an automatic right to inclusion in the main branch.

Publication of an IIP draft does not constitute validation. The states *Proposed*, *Draft*, *Candidate*, *Validated*, *Adopted*, and *Deprecated* are preliminary labels, not certifications currently in operation. No proposal should be marked *Validated* without evidence of independent testing against predefined criteria.

## Responsibility and Evolution of Governance

The community may later establish subject-area maintainers, appointment procedures, appeals rules, conflict-of-interest management, and criteria governing official versions. A potential independent, non-profit stewardship organisation remains a **hypothesis**, not an existing institution.

Original conception of the project and authorship of later contributions should remain visible. Historical attribution does not automatically entail a perpetual right of approval.

## Licensing Contributions

The proposed intention is to distribute **documents** under CC BY 4.0, with attribution of authors and changes. Before public activation, consent from the relevant rights holders must be obtained. Contributors must have the legal right to make their contributions available on these terms. Any software and data added in the future will require suitable, separate terms.
